WHO WE ARE

Unified Public Service Architecture

Historically, citizens had to travel across Hossana to different departments to obtain licenses, verify documents, and pay fees. Hossana Mesob One Digital Service Center gathers 12 separate government entities under one facility, powered by digital processing systems.

This center runs on a full digital framework, aligning with Ethiopia's National ID system (Fayda) to create an authenticated profile for citizens, enabling immediate and paperless public service.

Institutional Identity

Establishment Place Hossana, Central Ethiopia
Core Technologies Fayda Biometrics, QR Document Verification
Unified Departments 12 Government Units

Selection Criteria for Unified Offices

  • • Capacity for fast digital workflow adoption

NATIONAL STRATEGY

National Reform & Digitalization Strategy

Hossana Mesob One is established under the framework of Ethiopia's civil service reform program. The focus is to harmonize database infrastructure. By connecting trade, taxation, immigration, banking, and address registers to the National ID core database, we remove bureaucratic blockages.
